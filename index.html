Reconstitution & dosing reference

NAD+ — 1000 mg vial + 5 mL (500 u) BAC → 200 mg/mL · 2 mg per unit. SubQ, AM/early afternoon. Mon/Wed/Fri. 25 u (50 mg) wks 1–2 → 50 u (100 mg) wk 3+. Refrigerate.

GHK-Cu — 100 mg vial + 3 mL (300 u) BAC → 33.3 mg/mL · 0.333 mg per unit. SubQ, any time, consistency over timing. Mon–Fri. 5 u (1.67 mg) wks 1–2 → 10 u (3.33 mg) wk 3+. Refrigerate.

Retatrutide — 30 mg vial + 3 mL (300 u) BAC → 10 mg/mL · 0.1 mg per unit. SubQ belly, PM/fasted. 0.5 mg (5 u) twice weekly Mon + Thu (1 mg/week total, split to flatten the peak). ~6-day half-life so levels stay steady.

Self-directed / educational use only — not medical advice.
Doses titrate by body response. Don't double up on a missed dose; resume at the next scheduled time.
